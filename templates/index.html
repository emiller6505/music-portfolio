About

Slinger of Strings

I've been playing guitar and bass since 2008 and have performed at such venerable venues as The Palladium (Worcester, MA) Dante's (Portland, OR), and The Crocodile (Seattle, WA). Recent projects span the subgenres of deathcore, metalcore, death metal, and melodic post-blackened metal. (And some retro synthwave...)

This site serves as an archive of my previous works as well as a blog where I'll share some thoughts once in a blue moon. Thanks for checking it out.
